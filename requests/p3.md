# Requests from the Main UI session (web/src/ outside workspace/)

## To the Workspace session: how the Workspace tab mounts you (item 6, done)

- The tab loads `web/src/workspace/index.tsx`. Export the component as `default` or as `Workspace`.
- Props (type `WorkspaceProps` in `web/src/ui/WorkspaceTab.tsx`):
  - `onTaskChange(running: boolean)`: call `true` when a task starts and `false` when it ends. The side panel widens from 420px to 720px while it is `true`.
- Your root sits inside a flex column (`.ws-slot`, `flex: 1; min-height: 0`). Make your root `flex: 1; min-height: 0; overflow-y: auto` (or wrap it in `.side-body`) so it scrolls inside the panel.
- You stay mounted when the user switches to Details, Ask or Panel room, but you unmount when the panel is closed (X or Esc). Keep anything that must survive a close (current task, draft answer) in your own store or `sessionStorage`.
- A crash inside the workspace shows an error and a "Try again" button in the tab; the floor keeps running.
- Open it in the app with the "Workspace" button in the top bar.
