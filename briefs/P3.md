# P3 UI and flow

You are the **P3 UI and flow** session for **Lab Rotation**, our entry in today's
Multiplayer AI Hackathon (Finch, AWS Builder Loft, Mon Nov 23 2026). We submit by
**5:50 PM**. Nick (nick-devvv) supervises you; work without asking to continue.

**The product.** A flight simulator for an analyst's first weeks. A panel of AI
scientists (Dr. Vale the lab head, Ines the examiner, Sam the methods reviewer,
Telem the librarian, Riley the careless AI intern) hands the learner a small retail
dataset (customers, loyalty members, visits, spend, stores, regions; up to 3 tables,
a few hundred rows) and a claim to check. The data may hide one planted trap
(duplicated transactions, one region's spend in cents, a loyalty flag coded
backwards in the dictionary, a point-of-sale confounder, a hidden variable in a
second table) or none (a clean twin). The learner explores with a read-only SQL box,
profiles and charts, cites evidence, answers. The panel confers in a BAND room,
Ines shows the failing Bruin check, the verdict quotes the learner's own words, the
grade is sealed in a hash chain, a learner model (BKT + bandit) picks the next task.
The 2D lab floor (and a three.js view) shows agents walking, envelopes flying,
and a lab log with every event's reason.

## Your job
You own the app shell, the data layer and the panels.

- **Data layer** (`web/src/data/`): `contract.ts` (types mirroring the contract;
  keep it in sync), an API client for every route in `contract/api.md`, and an
  event store: subscribe to `GET /api/sessions/{id}/events` (SSE, `event: lab`),
  keep the event list, and derive state (agent places, phase, clock, transcript,
  whispers, checks, verdict, mastery, sponsor counts). Offline fallback: replay
  `/mock/mock_session.jsonl`. P1 and P2 consume this; agree shapes early.
- **App and flow** (`App.tsx`): start screen with the two modes (rotation, judge),
  then floor + workspace + side panels; the phases in `contract/api.md`.
- **Panels** (`web/src/ui/`): lab log (every event's `why`, filterable by agent);
  verdict card (score parts, the failing Bruin check beside the learner's evidence,
  checked quotes highlighted, ledger hash); BAND transcript (whispers hidden until
  the verdict, then opened) and the appeal view; mastery chart (recharts, before vs
  after); "beat the intern" card (Riley's answer after the learner's); sponsor strip
  under the floor counting each tool's calls live from `sponsor.call` events (BAND
  messages, Telem searches with trace links, Bruin checks run/failed, Runware voice
  lines and cost, Glasser calls); "ask any agent why" chat (`POST /ask`); audio
  playback of `voice.line` (fallback: browser speechSynthesis).
- Styles in `styles.css`. Clear, readable, a little playful; must look good on a
  projector.
